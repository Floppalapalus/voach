Welcome to Coach Analytics
This tool analyses your coaching sessions using AI — recording patterns, questions asked, praise given, and tactical language — and gives you personalised feedback aligned with the US Soccer Framework.
📊
What gets measured
After each upload you'll receive a Framework Profile (e.g. Developing Player-Centred Coach), metric bars vs your age-group targets, question quality breakdown, and one focused area to improve each session.
📽
5 session types supported
Training, Team Meeting, Game, Half-time, and Post-game each have different benchmarks — a game coach is expected to be more directive; a team meeting coach should ask more questions. Choose the right type for accurate feedback.
🚀
You're ready to go
Record your next session on your phone, upload it here, and your report will be ready in a few minutes. The more sessions you upload, the better your trend analysis becomes.
