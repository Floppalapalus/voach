What gets measured

Six behaviours that define player-centred coaching

Every metric maps directly to the US Soccer Player Development Framework and NCFC Youth guidelines.

🔍
Discovery Questions
How often you ask players to think for themselves instead of telling them the answer.
⚖️
Questioning Balance
Your ask-vs-tell ratio. The goal is more questions, fewer instructions.
👏

How often you reinforce what's working with specific, named praise.

Your praise-to-correction balance. Players learn faster in positive environments.
